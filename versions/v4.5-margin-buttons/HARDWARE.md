# V4.5 hardware — V4 board, no pump, + MARGIN buttons

The keyboard, feedback LEDs, game-select switch, restart button and buzzer are all
as documented in [../v4-water-pump/HARDWARE.md](../v4-water-pump/HARDWARE.md).
This page covers the two deltas: the relay pair and pump are **gone**, and two
MARGIN buttons are **new**.

## Pin map (Arduino Nano) — changes in bold

| Pin | Direction | Role |
|---|---|---|
| A0–A6 | analog in | 7 lemon touch keys, 220 Ω in series |
| D2 / D3 | out | red / green feedback LED |
| D4 | in | GAME SELECT SPDT switch |
| **D5 / D6** | — | **unused — V4's relay pair and water pump are off this board** |
| D7 | in | RESTART button (active-HIGH) |
| D8 | out | passive buzzer (key notes, tunes, and the penalty groan) |
| **D10** | **in** | **MARGIN + button — raise the touch margin (less sensitive)** |
| **D11** | **in** | **MARGIN − button — lower the touch margin (more sensitive)** |
| D13 | out | on-board LED, blinks per bit-banged melody note |
| 5V | — | hand-held alligator clip (the player) |

Free after this revision: D5, D6, D9 and D12 — four pins that
[V5](../v5-led-bar/HARDWARE.md) puts to work in its ten-LED bar (which is also why
V5 had to move game-select to A7).

## What came off: the relay pair and the pump

V4's 2-channel relay module and the water pump are removed entirely — module,
tubing, pump and both signal lines. Nothing replaces them; D5/D6 are simply not
wired.

The **penalty still exists**, it is just audible instead of wet: a miss from note 7
onward (`PENALTY_FROM_STEP = 7`) plays `NOTE_D1` for `PENALTY_MS = 1000` ms and
increments the fail counter, and ten penalties (`MAX_FAILS = 10`) still end the game
with the death tune until RESTART. The firmware has no relay code left at all —
`RELAY_1`/`RELAY_2`, `pumpOff()` and `firePump()` are gone, replaced by
`playPenalty()`.

One consequence worth noting: with no inductive load and no relay module drawing
from the 5 V rail, this board is comfortably USB-powered — V4's pump was the reason
its supply had to be sized at all.

## The two new buttons

Wired exactly like RESTART — active-HIGH, so each button needs its own pulldown:

```
 5V ──[button]──┬── D10 (MARGIN +)      same for D11 (MARGIN −)
                └──[10 kΩ]── GND
```

- Each press nudges a **manual offset** by `MARGIN_STEP = 10`, applied on top of
  every key's auto-calibrated margin (so the relative per-key differences from
  calibration are preserved), bounded to `[-120, +400]` with an effective per-key
  margin floor of 8.
- The buttons are **hardware-only**: the Velxio build uses D11 for the buzzer and
  has no pins left, so the emulation compiles without them.
- ⚠️ D11 is also the AVR's OC2A pin. That is harmless as an input here, but it is
  the reason the *emulation* build puts the buzzer there — don't wire a buzzer to
  D11 on this board.

## Noise-adaptive calibration (firmware, no wiring)

```
threshold[key] = baseline[key] + clamp(max(40, 3 × noise[key])) + manualOffset
                                                       ↑ capped so the total ≤ 900
```

`calibrate()` measures each key's floating baseline **and** its peak-to-mean noise,
at boot and on **every RESTART**. A quiet bench gives a tight threshold; a
laptop-charger-noisy line gives a wider one automatically. Hands off the lemons
while it runs.
